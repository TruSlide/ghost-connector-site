Lightwright

A Claude connector that builds Destiny 2 loadouts from the gear you already own.

Status: in development. It is not released yet.

What it will do

  • Ask Claude for a loadout for an activity and playstyle, such as "Voidwalker, Devour and grenade explosions, for a raid."
  • Propose a loadout from items you own, with exact stat totals from the game's data.
  • List any missing items and where they come from.
  • Move items between characters and the vault, pull from the postmaster, and equip gear or a full loadout, after you approve each change.

How your data is handled

  • You sign in with your own Bungie account. Lightwright never sees your password.
  • Access tokens are stored on your own computer, not on a server run by this project.
  • Nothing changes in your account until you confirm it. Every change shows a preview first and is logged locally.
  • Nothing is deleted or sold.
